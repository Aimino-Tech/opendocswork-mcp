# Density and Spacing

Use one density mode per surface. Dense dashboards may use 8/12/16px rhythm; standard product screens 8/16/24/32; marketing can be more spacious. Do not mix arbitrary gaps such as 13/19/27px without a system.

Dense does not mean cramped: keep hit targets usable, separate unrelated actions, and reserve whitespace around decision points. Repeated list/table rows should be compact; forms and destructive confirmations deserve more breathing room.
