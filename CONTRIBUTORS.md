## Contributor Recognition

We believe in recognizing contributions of all kinds — code, documentation, skills, design, community support.

- **Contributor** — 1+ merged PR
- **Active Contributor** — 5+ merged PRs
- **Core Contributor** — 20+ merged PRs / Maintainer nomination
- **Community Ambassador** — 3+ community contributions (talks, blog posts, skills)

See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) for how to get started.
